# Stellaryn community code of conduct

Stellaryn welcomes contributions and technical disagreements from people of different backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive and specific. Discuss code, evidence and behavior—not personal characteristics.
- Assume questions and good-faith mistakes are opportunities to improve documentation.
- Be careful with security reports, private keys, proprietary contract source and third-party licenses.
- Give credit for upstream sources, fixtures and prior work.
- Accept maintainer decisions even when there is disagreement; seek resolution with evidence, not harassment.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory insults, sexualized remarks, disclosure of someone's private information, spam, and repeated disruptive or hostile comments are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Scope and reporting

This code applies to public issues, pull requests, documentation discussions, and other project community channels. Flag conduct concerns to repository maintainers through a **private** contact channel where available. Do not publish sensitive accusations or personal details in public issues. If no private channel is available, request one without sharing sensitive details.

Maintainers may edit or close disruptive issues, decline contributions, or restrict participation to protect the community. No response-time guarantee is offered.
